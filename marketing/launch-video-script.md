## Production notes

- Footage: shul families (with permission) beats stock — even phone-shot
  clips in good window light work with this script. If using stock, keep it
  visibly frum-appropriate.
- The site itself is the best prop: record the actual signup and check-in on
  a phone (the flows are built and clickable) so the video *shows* how easy
  it is rather than claiming it.
- Keep every shot under ~2.5 seconds; the warmth comes from light and faces,
  not slow pacing.
- End-card URL should stay on screen at least 3 full seconds.
